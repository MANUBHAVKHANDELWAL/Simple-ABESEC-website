About ABES Engineering College

ABES Engineering College is a private higher-education institution located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, established in the year 2000.

Overview & Academics

Programs Offered: Undergraduate (B.Tech) and Postgraduate (M.Tech, MCA, MBA) courses. B.Tech is the flagship program, with a major focus on emerging disciplines like Computer Science, AI/ML, and Data Science.

Affiliation: Affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, and approved by AICTE.

Campus: The campus has academic blocks, departmental labs, a central library, sports complexes, swimming pool, and multiple canteens.

Admissions & Fees

Eligibility: B.Tech admissions generally require passing 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
